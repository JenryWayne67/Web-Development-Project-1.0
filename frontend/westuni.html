info About West Yangon University

West Yangon University (WYU) is a public liberal arts and sciences university located in Htantabin, Yangon, Myanmar. Established to provide higher education opportunities for students in the western part of the Yangon region, WYU offers bachelor's and master's degree programs in common liberal arts, sciences, and law disciplines.

The university is known for its sprawling, green campus and its commitment to academic excellence in the humanities and fundamental sciences, serving as a vital educational hub for the local community and beyond.
