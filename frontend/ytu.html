Yangon Technological University (YTU) is Myanmar's premier institution for engineering education. Established in 1961, it has a long-standing tradition of academic excellence and has produced many of the nation's top engineers and technologists. The university offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to meet international standards.
