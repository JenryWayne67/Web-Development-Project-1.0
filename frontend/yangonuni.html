About University of Yangon

The University of Yangon is Myanmar's oldest and most prestigious university. Established in 1920, it has been the focal point of Myanmar's modern academic and political history. Situated on a beautiful, expansive campus in Kamayut Township, the university remains a symbol of academic excellence, offering a comprehensive range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs primarily focused on the Arts, Sciences, and Law.
