About University of Computer Studies (UCSY)

The University of Computer Studies, Yangon (UCSY) is the premier university for computer science and information technology in Myanmar. Established to foster technological advancement, UCSY offers a rigorous curriculum designed to prepare students for the rapidly evolving tech landscape. With state-of-the-art facilities and a commitment to research and innovation, UCSY stands as a beacon for aspiring IT professionals across the nation.
